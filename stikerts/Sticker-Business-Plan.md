# Custom Sticker Side Hustle

**Business Plan — Bin Stickers, Kids' Name Labels & Vinyl Decals**

*Prepared July 2026 · Starting configuration: Tier 1 equipment*

## 1. Executive Summary

This plan sets out a low-overhead side hustle producing custom vinyl stickers — starting with bin/wheelie bin number stickers, personalised kids' name labels, and simple vinyl decals (water bottles, laptops), sold via eBay and/or a personal website. The business will start unregistered for VAT, staying below the UK's £90,000 VAT threshold, and will launch with a Tier 1 equipment setup (current-generation inkjet printer + cutting machine + hand-applied lamination), which is confirmed as the genuine small-business production standard for this market, not a compromise.

## 2. Market Overview

**Bin Stickers**

An active UK market with multiple established sellers (Etsy, Stickythings, UK Sign Shop, Discount Sticker Printing). Demand is recurring — every household with a wheelie bin is a potential customer, and demand renews naturally as people move house.

**Kids' Name Labels / Clothing Labels**

A larger, well-established niche. Brands like Stikins and My Nametags are built entirely around this product, with Stikins alone holding over 13,000 five-star reviews. Demand is strongly seasonal (back-to-school peak) but highly repeat-purchase, since parents return yearly as children grow and school years change.

**Vinyl Decals (water bottles, laptops, general)**

One of Etsy's most consistently strong sticker categories. Top-selling listings report 10,000–50,000+ lifetime sales, at price points of $2.50–$5 per sticker or $12–$25 for multi-packs. Broad, evergreen demand not tied to a single season.

**Competitive Landscape**

Fragmented across many small sellers rather than dominated by a handful of large players — a realistic entry point for a new, well-priced, well-presented shop rather than a market that requires beating an established giant.

## 4. Legal & Regulatory

- VAT registration is not required while turnover stays below the UK threshold of £90,000 (2025/26 and 2026/27 tax years) — this is a rolling 12-month figure, not a fixed tax year, so turnover should be tracked continuously as the business grows.
- VAT status is tied to the seller's total turnover, not to the platform used — eBay, Etsy, and a personal website are all treated the same way for this purpose.
- This document is not legal or tax advice — confirm registration thresholds and any other obligations (e.g. business insurance, product safety labelling for children's items) directly with HMRC or a qualified adviser before trading.

## 7. Packaging Strategy

Every order includes a thank-you card as standard. Packaging approach varies by product to match its use case and physical requirements:

| Product | Approach | Components | Cost/order |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decals & name labels | Premium (gift feel) | C6 kraft envelope + tissue wrap + thank-you card + self-made seal sticker | ≈ £0.34 |
| Bin stickers | Rigid, functional | C4 board-backed "do not bend" envelope + thank-you card + self-made backing card | ≈ £0.23–0.25 |
| Packaging labels (B2B) | Simple, professional | C6 kraft envelope + thank-you card | ≈ £0.21 |

Bin stickers require a board-backed envelope rather than a standard kraft mailer — at the large size (30x20cm), a normal envelope would crease or fold the sticker sheet in transit. This is a functional requirement, not an upsell.

Two packaging elements cost next to nothing extra: the seal sticker and backing cards are produced on the same printer/cutter already used for the products themselves, using offcut vinyl and cardstock rather than purchased pre-made items.

## 8. Shipping Strategy

Competitor research shows a consistent pattern: free standard (untracked) shipping baked into the item price is the default across nearly every seller in this space (Stikins, wheelie bin sticker Etsy shops, My Nametags), with tracked delivery offered as a paid checkout upgrade. No competitor found offers a genuinely guaranteed next-day service — this is an open gap.

Stickers and labels are thin and flat, qualifying for Royal Mail's cheapest postal format (Large Letter) rather than Small Parcel — packaging should be kept flat to preserve this rate, since Small Parcel format roughly doubles postage cost.

| Service | Format | Price (2026) | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2nd Class (untracked) | Large Letter | ~85p–£1.55 | 2-3 working days |
| Tracked 48 | Large Letter | £2.75 | 2-3 working days, tracked |
| Tracked 24 | Large Letter | £3.65 | Aims next working day, tracked (not guaranteed) |
| Special Delivery Guaranteed by 1pm | Any | from £8.75–9.95 | Genuinely guaranteed next working day, signed, compensation up to £750 |

Recommended three-tier shipping menu:

- **Standard** (free, built into item price): 2nd Class Large Letter, untracked, 2-3 days. Matches competitor default and supports "free shipping" visibility in Etsy/eBay search.
- **Tracked** (paid upgrade, e.g. +£1.50–2.00): Tracked 48, 2-3 days with tracking.
- **Next Working Day** (paid upgrade, e.g. +£3.00): Tracked 24. Note this "aims for" rather than guarantees next-day delivery — worth stating honestly in listings, since only Special Delivery is a true guarantee.
- **Special Delivery Guaranteed** (from £8.75–9.95) is disproportionate to a single £6-10 item — offer only for bundled/higher-value orders (£20+), not as a standard tier.

Shipping is a per-order cost, not a per-item cost — a bundled order (e.g. 4 bin stickers or a mixed decal pack) still ships in a single Large Letter for the same postage cost, which is a further reason bundling improves margin (see Section 9).

## 9. Break-Even Analysis (Revised — includes packaging and shipping cost)

Two scenarios are shown: a single-item order (conservative, worst case) and a bundled order (more realistic once the business is established).

- Startup cost: £670–800 (equipment only, includes cold roll laminator as core)
- Single-item order, eBay: £6.00 sale − £1.08 fees − £1.50 materials/packaging − £1.20 standard shipping (absorbed) = **£2.22 profit** → break-even ≈ 302–360 sales
- Single-item order, own site: £6.00 − £0.32 fees − £1.50 − £1.20 = **£2.98 profit** → break-even ≈ 225–269 sales
- Bundled order (~£9 average), eBay: £9.00 − £1.17 fees − £2.00 materials/packaging (shared across items) − £1.20 shipping (same single postage cost) = **£4.63 profit** → break-even ≈ 145–173 sales

The gap between the single-item and bundled scenarios is the clearest evidence yet that bundling is the priority lever for this business: shipping and packaging costs are largely fixed per order, so spreading them across more items per order has an outsized effect on profit. Paid shipping upgrades (Tracked, Next Working Day) are priced to roughly cover their extra postage cost plus a small margin, not as a profit centre.

## 10. Production Workflow

Confirmed as the genuine professional small-business method for waterproof vinyl stickers:

1. Design the artwork, laid out multiple-per-sheet (4-up for bin stickers at 100x140mm; see below for why this is no longer designed in Cricut Design Space).
2. Print onto printable vinyl using the pigment-ink printer (waterproof once dry — critical for durability).
3. Apply laminate using the cold roll laminator (squeegee by hand as backup), before cutting, to protect the print and make it water/scratch resistant.
4. **Cut — method depends on product shape (updated July 2026):**
   - **Bin stickers (plain rounded rectangle):** straight-trim the A4 sheet into 4 stickers with the guillotine, round the corners with the corner rounder punch, then nick a short (~1cm) peel-start line through the vinyl + laminate (not the backing) with the Slice 00200 safety cutter. This avoids Cricut Print Then Cut entirely — its registration-mark size limit doesn't allow a full edge-to-edge A4 sheet in one pass, which the original Cricut-based plan for this product didn't account for. No Cricut is needed to launch this product — see Section 5.
   - **Products with an actual contoured shape** (e.g. novelty/monogram water bottle & laptop decals — see Growth Plan): would need a Cricut Explore 5's Print-Then-Cut feature, which reads registration marks and cuts precisely around the laminated design, if/when those products launch. Note the same size limit applies here too — keep contoured designs within roughly 7.2 x 10.62in (182.9 x 269.7mm) on A4, and confirm the actual limit for your Design Space version before finalising layouts.
5. Weed excess material (contoured products only), package (per the packaging strategy above), and ship.

Estimated time for a 20-sticker batch: 45–70 minutes, with weeding/cutting taking the largest share. Time per batch compresses for repeat designs (same layout, personalised text/number swapped in). The bin sticker manual cutting steps (trim, round, nick) haven't yet been timed at volume — worth timing your first few real batches to sanity-check this estimate still holds for the new method.

## 12. Growth Plan

- **Phase 1 (Months 1–3)**: Launch with 4 starter products (bin stickers, name labels, water bottle/laptop decals, small business packaging labels) on Tier 1 equipment. Focus on hitting break-even, prioritising bundle listings given their outsized margin benefit.

**Planned — Premium Bin Sticker Tier (next, immediately after current bin sticker launch)**

Once the standard 100×140mm bin sticker is live and selling, introduce a second, higher-priced tier of the same product — no new product category, just a "Standard" vs "Premium" split. Options identified so far, roughly in order of ease of implementation:

- **Bundled extras (cheapest to add, no new sourcing):** matching recycling bin / food caddy sticker; a spare/replacement sticker included; a small "bin day reminder" magnet or sticker for inside a cupboard — none of these are offered by competitors identified in the Competitor Research file, so this may be a genuine differentiator.
- **Material/finish tier:** matte vs gloss lamination as a simple visual differentiator; double lamination on the premium tier for extra scratch/UV resistance.
- **Holographic or metallic-effect vinyl (premium only):** confirmed compatible with the existing inkjet + Cricut workflow, no new equipment needed. Real cost impact: roughly **£2.24/page vs £0.65/page** for standard vinyl (~3.5x), so premium pricing needs to reflect this. Caveat to test before committing: house-number designs are typically bold, solid-fill text, which tends to hide the holographic/metallic shimmer effect rather than show it off — mock up an actual number design on the material and check in person before finalising this as a premium differentiator.
- **Personalisation depth:** number-only (standard) vs number + street name + decorative border (premium) — several Etsy competitors in the Competitor Research file already price this way. **Update (Aug 2026): already built.** D02 (Regency Double Flourish) and D03 (Manor Frame Classic) — both "Proof approved" — already deliver exactly this number + street name + decorative border combination; see `bin_sticker_products_gallery_data.md` for the finished designs. Note both are the landscape 140×100mm shape, not this section's 100×140mm portrait default — worth deciding whether that's part of the premium differentiation or just an implementation detail to reconcile.
- **Service-based tiers (no material cost):** faster dispatch as a premium perk; reserve the strongest durability-guarantee wording (see The Range's 10-year guarantee, flagged in Competitor Research as the bar to match) for the premium tier specifically, tied to the better laminate.

Not yet decided: exact Standard vs Premium price points, and which 2–3 of the above to actually launch with rather than listing all of them. Revisit once the current material test (Bin-Sticker-Material-Test-Plan.md) and the base bin sticker price/size are both finalised, since the premium tier's pricing depends on the standard tier being locked first.

- **Phase 2 (Months 3–6)**: Introduce bundles to lift average order value and speed up break-even; expand designs within proven categories.
- **Phase 3 (6+ months, if volume justifies)**: Reinvest profits into Tier 3 equipment (UV DTF printer, e.g. eufyMake E1) for improved durability and to open new product categories (mugs, bottles, hard surfaces).

**Planned — Original Character Design Sets (for decal/label product lines, timing TBD)**

Licensed characters (Disney, Harry Potter, etc.) are not usable — copyright/trademark protected and actively enforced by marketplaces, and a renamed/reskinned close visual copy is still infringement, not a workaround. Instead: develop **10 sets of original character designs** (e.g. a "wizards & magical creatures" or "animated characters" theme, own designs — not visual copies of existing IP) with polished reference images, for use across relevant product lines (most likely water bottle/laptop decals). Not yet scheduled against a specific phase — added here as a tracked future task.

**Planned — Polished illustrated artwork for premium bin sticker designs (timing TBD)**

Decided (July 2026): use **Midjourney + Vectorizer.ai** for the 6 icon-based designs (floral, recycling, house silhouette, vintage postmark, corner flourish, paw print) — the other 4 designs (classic, minimal, reverse-block, split-panel) don't use an icon and don't need this.

Why this path over a pure icon generator (Recraft AI, etc.): more illustrated/hand-drawn feel, which is the specific gap identified versus the bestselling Etsy pet-silhouette listings. Licensing: any paid Midjourney plan (Basic, $10/mo) gives full commercial-use rights for a business under $1M annual revenue, which comfortably covers this side hustle.

**Workflow (see chat history for the exact 6 prompts):**
1. Generate each icon on a plain white background, using a consistent style suffix and the same `--seed` across all 6 so the set feels cohesive, not 6 unrelated styles.
2. Crop/upscale the chosen result from Midjourney's 4-grid.
3. Run through Vectorizer.ai to get a clean file with a transparent background. Export as a high-resolution PNG (~2000px on the long edge — comfortably over 300dpi at this sticker's print size; true vector/SVG precision isn't needed at 100×140mm, so this skips reportlab's lack of native SVG support entirely).
4. Drop the PNG at the path already wired up in `bin_sticker.py`'s `ICON_ASSETS` dict (e.g. `assets/icons/paw_icon.png`). The script automatically uses the illustrated artwork once the file exists there, and falls back to the built-in vector shape if it doesn't — already built and tested, no further code change needed when the artwork is ready.

Cost/time: ~$10 for one month of Midjourney + a few minutes per icon generating/picking favourites (6 icons total) + Vectorizer.ai's free tier or ~$4/month covers the conversions.

## 13. Risks & Considerations

- Sticker market is competitive — differentiation through design quality, niche focus, and customer service matters.
- Ink/material choice is critical to durability — dye-based ink or skipping lamination risks poor-quality, smearing products and negative reviews.
- Turnover must be tracked against the VAT threshold on a rolling 12-month basis as the business scales.
- Seasonal products (back-to-school labels, wedding favours) will see demand fluctuate — a mixed product line smooths this out.
- Royal Mail prices change periodically (last updated 7 April 2026 in this plan) — shipping charges should be reviewed against current rates before finalising listing prices.
