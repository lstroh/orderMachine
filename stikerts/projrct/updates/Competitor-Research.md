## Overall takeaways across all 4 products

- **Bin stickers & name labels** have the clearest "quality bar" to match (weatherproof/waterproof durability claims, often with named guarantees).
- **Water bottle/laptop decals** are the most saturated — success here depends more on design/style differentiation than on production quality alone.
- **Packaging labels** reward **no-minimum-order + fast turnaround** positioning, which is a natural fit for a small home-based Tier 1 setup.
- Across all categories, **personalisation** (names, house numbers, custom text) is the common thread that commands better pricing than generic designs — worth leaning into across your whole product line.
