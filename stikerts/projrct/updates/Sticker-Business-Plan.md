# Custom Sticker Side Hustle

**Business Plan — Bin Stickers, Kids' Name Labels & Vinyl Decals**

*Trading name: **Doorstep Studio** (confirmed Sep 2026; domain doorstepstudio.co.uk). The earlier working name "Kerbside Craft Co." is retired.*

*Prepared July 2026 · Last revised September 2026 (synced to cost sheet StickerBinStickersCosts_v4.xlsx) · Starting configuration: Tier 1 equipment*

## 1. Executive Summary

This plan sets out a low-overhead side hustle producing custom vinyl stickers — starting with bin/wheelie bin number stickers, personalised kids' name labels, and simple vinyl decals (water bottles, laptops), sold via eBay first, then Etsy, then Amazon (own website at doorstepstudio.co.uk to follow — timing TBD). The business will start unregistered for VAT, staying below the UK's £90,000 VAT threshold, and will launch with a Tier 1 equipment setup (current-generation inkjet printer + cutting machine + cold roll laminator), which is confirmed as the genuine small-business production standard for this market, not a compromise.

## 2. Market Overview

**Bin Stickers**

An active UK market with multiple established sellers (Etsy, Stickythings, UK Sign Shop, Discount Sticker Printing). Demand is recurring — every household with a wheelie bin is a potential customer, and demand renews naturally as people move house.

**Kids' Name Labels / Clothing Labels**

A larger, well-established niche. Brands like Stikins and My Nametags are built entirely around this product, with Stikins alone holding over 13,000 five-star reviews. Demand is strongly seasonal (back-to-school peak) but highly repeat-purchase, since parents return yearly as children grow and school years change.

**Vinyl Decals (water bottles, laptops, general)**

One of Etsy's most consistently strong sticker categories. Top-selling listings report 10,000–50,000+ lifetime sales, at price points of $2.50–$5 per sticker or $12–$25 for multi-packs. Broad, evergreen demand not tied to a single season.

**Competitive Landscape**

Fragmented across many small sellers rather than dominated by a handful of large players — a realistic entry point for a new, well-priced, well-presented shop rather than a market that requires beating an established giant.

## 7. Packaging Strategy

Every order includes a thank-you card as standard. Two printed variants, made on the same printer/cutter from offcut cardstock: **Standard** (eBay and Etsy orders) — thank-you, care instructions and a neutral feedback ask, no discount code; **Website** (own-site orders only) — same plus a 10% code. No review language at all on any Amazon card. Never pair a discount with a review request or ask for a positive/5-star review. Reasons: eBay restricts promoting sales outside eBay and coded coupons need an eBay Shop subscription; Etsy codes must resolve on Etsy; Amazon treats insert review requests as manipulation risk.

Packaging approach varies by product to match its use case and physical requirements:

| Product | Approach | Components | Cost/order |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decals & name labels | Premium (gift feel) | C6 kraft envelope + tissue wrap + thank-you card + self-made seal sticker | ≈ £0.34 |
| Bin stickers (140×100mm, 4-pack) | Rigid, functional | A6/C6 board-backed "do not bend" envelope (114×162mm) + thank-you card + self-made backing card | ≈ £0.25 (sourced, box-of-250 scale, All Colour Envelopes). The cost sheet also counts the thank-you card separately (£0.16) — confirm the £0.25 does not already include it. The R&D test pack (Double Dragon, 50) works out at £0.147 each — reconcile which supplier will be used |
| Packaging labels (B2B) | Simple, professional | C6 kraft envelope + thank-you card | ≈ £0.21 |

Bin stickers require a board-backed envelope rather than a standard kraft mailer, to prevent creasing/folding in transit. This is a functional requirement, not an upsell. **Note:** the bin sticker size was reduced from the original 30×20cm to 140×100mm (see StickerBinStickersCosts_v4.xlsx, "Bin Stickers 100x140 4pk" sheet), so the envelope size dropped accordingly from **C4 to A6/C6** — smaller and cheaper than the original C4 assumption, though the per-unit cost happens to land in the same ≈£0.23–0.25 range. **A6/C6 applies to the 140×100mm baseline only:** the Medium (202×140mm) and Large (280×200mm) tiers (Section 12) are too big for it and would need roughly C5 and C4 board-backed envelopes respectively — not yet sourced or costed. The A6/C6 choice is confirmed but still to be validated in the post by Test 6 (transit stress, see Bin-Sticker-Material-Test-Plan.md).

**Open question — multi-item orders:** not yet resolved how to package orders containing more than one product, or more than one set of the same product. A single A6/C6 envelope may not accommodate multiple packs within Royal Mail's Large Letter thickness limit — needs a dedicated decision (see Section 8 for the thickness constraint this interacts with).

Two packaging elements cost next to nothing extra: the seal sticker and backing cards are produced on the same printer/cutter already used for the products themselves, using offcut vinyl and cardstock rather than purchased pre-made items.

## 8. Shipping Strategy

Competitor research shows a consistent pattern: free standard (untracked) shipping baked into the item price is the default across nearly every seller in this space (Stikins, wheelie bin sticker Etsy shops, My Nametags), with tracked delivery offered as a paid checkout upgrade. No competitor found offers a genuinely guaranteed next-day service — this is an open gap.

Stickers and labels are thin and flat, qualifying for Royal Mail's Large Letter format (up to 25mm thick) rather than Small Parcel — packaging should be kept flat to preserve this rate, since Small Parcel format roughly doubles postage cost. If the packed order measures under 5mm it may qualify for the cheaper Letter rate; this is being measured in Test 6 (the backing-card comparator measured 4mm and 36g in Aug 2026 — Letter-eligible but close to the limit; the board-backed envelope is not yet measured).

| Service | Format | Price (2026) | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2nd Class (untracked) | Letter (up to 5mm thick, 100g) | £0.91 (cost sheet figure — confirm in Click & Drop) | 2-3 working days |
| 2nd Class (untracked) | Large Letter | £1.55 (up to 100g), £1.90 (up to 250g) | 2-3 working days |
| Tracked 48 | Large Letter | £2.75 | 2-3 working days, tracked |
| Tracked 24 | Large Letter | £3.65 | Aims next working day, tracked (not guaranteed) |
| Special Delivery Guaranteed by 1pm | Any | from £8.75–9.95 | Genuinely guaranteed next working day, signed, compensation up to £750 |

*Correction (Sep 2026): the earlier "~85p" figure was a Letter rate, not Large Letter, which starts at £1.55. The cost sheet uses £0.91 for Letter — confirm against the current Royal Mail price list. The cost sheet currently assumes the Letter rate for the bin sticker 4-pack; that depends on Test 6 confirming the packed order is under 5mm.*

Recommended three-tier shipping menu:

- **Standard** (free, built into item price): 2nd Class untracked, 2-3 days — Letter rate (£0.91) if the packed order is under 5mm and 100g, otherwise Large Letter (£1.55). Matches competitor default and supports "free shipping" visibility in Etsy/eBay search.
- **Tracked** (paid upgrade, e.g. +£1.50–2.00): Tracked 48, 2-3 days with tracking.
- **Next Working Day** (paid upgrade, e.g. +£3.00): Tracked 24. Note this "aims for" rather than guarantees next-day delivery — worth stating honestly in listings, since only Special Delivery is a true guarantee.
- **Special Delivery Guaranteed** (from £8.75–9.95) is disproportionate to a single £6-10 item — offer only for bundled/higher-value orders (£20+), not as a standard tier.

eBay cannot charge different postage per variation within one listing, so for the Medium and Large tiers any extra postage must be built into the item price rather than charged separately.

Shipping is a per-order cost, not a per-item cost — a bundled order (e.g. 4 bin stickers or a mixed decal pack) still ships in a single Large Letter for the same postage cost, which is a further reason bundling improves margin (see Section 9).

**Volume scenarios (from the overhead sheet):** conservative 10 stickers/month (2.5 packs), base 40 (10 packs), capacity ceiling ≈115 (about 29 packs, at one production hour a week). At the Letter rate the monthly result after overhead is about **−£81, −£75 and −£60** respectively — none of the modelled volumes covers overhead at £4.

**What would change this:**
- **Price.** Profit per pack before overhead at the Letter rate: £3 → −£0.03, £4 → £0.80, £5 → £1.63, £6 → £2.46 (eBay fees scale with price, apart from the flat 30p). The zero-profit price is about £3.03 at the Letter rate and £3.80 at Large Letter.
- **Postage tier.** Letter vs Large Letter is worth £0.64 a pack — the single biggest cost lever, and it hangs on Test 6 (the backing-card comparator measured 4mm against the 5mm limit).
- **Overhead.** Placeholder subscriptions and the virtual office (£20.83/month) are the largest fixed items to challenge.
- **Bundling.** Postage, envelope, address label and thank-you card are per order, so more items per order lifts profit disproportionately. Bundle scenarios are not yet modelled in the cost sheet.

The Medium and Large tiers cost about £1.59 and £3.19 per sticker before overhead (Section 12); their prices are not set yet. Paid shipping upgrades (Tracked, Next Working Day) are priced to roughly cover their extra postage plus a small margin, not as a profit centre.

## 10. Production Workflow

Confirmed as the genuine professional small-business method for waterproof vinyl stickers:

1. Generate the artwork (bin_sticker.py per-size scripts for bin stickers; Cricut Design Space for other products), laid out multiple-per-sheet with corner tick marks.
2. Print onto printable vinyl using the pigment-ink printer (waterproof once dry — critical for durability). **Bin sticker material: Cricut Printable Waterproof Sticker Set – A4 (20 ct), White (vinyl + matched laminate) — chosen Sep 2026.** Cricut renders dark saturated colours such as navy as near-black (Test Plan, Addendum 5), so accent colours must be checked before they are offered.
3. Apply laminate using the cold roll laminator (squeegee by hand as backup), before cutting, to protect the print and make it water/scratch resistant.
4. Cut on the Cricut Explore 5 in **cut-only mode for all size tiers** (fixed rounded-rectangle outlines at known coordinates, no registration marks). Square the printed sheet on the mat by hand against the printed corner tick marks. All three tiers' cut areas (about 200–202mm × 280mm) fit the Explore 5's 12×12 mat (292.1mm maximum cut area): Small (140×100mm, 4-up), Medium (202×140mm, 2-up stacked) and Large (280×200mm, 1-up).
5. Trim the kiss-cut sheet into separate backed stickers on the guillotine (and cut thank-you cards to A6), remove waste material, package (per the packaging strategy above), and ship.

*This replaces both Print-Then-Cut (its registration-mark area is too small for a full A4 sheet) and the earlier corner punch + Slice nick workflow; the guillotine stays for trimming. Kiss-cut depth (through vinyl and laminate, not the backing) needs test cuts on the actual stock.*

Estimated time for a 20-sticker batch: 45–70 minutes (estimate predates the cut-only workflow — re-time once tested), with cutting taking the largest share. Time per batch compresses for repeat designs (same layout, personalised text/number swapped in).

**Planned — Premium Bin Sticker Tier (next, immediately after current bin sticker launch)**

Once the standard 100×140mm bin sticker is live and selling, introduce a second, higher-priced tier of the same product — no new product category, just a "Standard" vs "Premium" split. Options identified so far, roughly in order of ease of implementation:

- **Bundled extras (cheapest to add, no new sourcing):** matching recycling bin / food caddy sticker; a spare/replacement sticker included; a small "bin day reminder" magnet or sticker for inside a cupboard — none of these are offered by competitors identified in the Competitor Research file, so this may be a genuine differentiator.
- **Material/finish tier:** matte vs gloss lamination as a simple visual differentiator; double lamination on the premium tier for extra scratch/UV resistance.
- **Holographic or metallic-effect vinyl (premium only):** confirmed compatible with the existing inkjet + Cricut workflow, no new equipment needed. Real cost impact: roughly **£2.24/page vs £0.65/page** for standard vinyl (~3.5x), so premium pricing needs to reflect this. Caveat to test before committing: house-number designs are typically bold, solid-fill text, which tends to hide the holographic/metallic shimmer effect rather than show it off — mock up an actual number design on the material and check in person before finalising this as a premium differentiator.
- **Personalisation depth:** number-only (standard) vs number + street name + decorative border (premium) — several Etsy competitors in the Competitor Research file already price this way.
- **Service-based tiers (no material cost):** faster dispatch as a premium perk; reserve the strongest durability-guarantee wording (see The Range's 10-year guarantee, flagged in Competitor Research as the bar to match) for the premium tier specifically, tied to the better laminate.

On eBay the bundled extras are offered as an add-on option inside the core listing (Section 11) rather than as a separate bundle listing.

Not yet decided: exact Standard vs Premium price points, and which 2–3 of the above to actually launch with rather than listing all of them. Revisit once the current material test (Bin-Sticker-Material-Test-Plan.md) and the base bin sticker price/size are both finalised, since the premium tier's pricing depends on the standard tier being locked first.

- **Phase 2 (Months 3–6)**: Introduce bundles to lift average order value and speed up break-even; expand designs within proven categories.
- **Phase 3 (6+ months, if volume justifies)**: Reinvest profits into Tier 3 equipment (UV DTF printer, e.g. eufyMake E1) for improved durability and to open new product categories (mugs, bottles, hard surfaces).

## 13. Risks & Considerations

- Sticker market is competitive — differentiation through design quality, niche focus, and customer service matters.
- Ink/material choice is critical to durability — dye-based ink or skipping lamination risks poor-quality, smearing products and negative reviews.
- Turnover must be tracked against the VAT threshold on a rolling 12-month basis as the business scales.
- Seasonal products (back-to-school labels, wedding favours) will see demand fluctuate — a mixed product line smooths this out.
- Unit margin at £4 is thin: about £0.80 a pack before overhead at the Letter rate, and £0.16 at Large Letter (Section 9). The postage tier (Test 6), the price and the fixed overhead all need settling before launch volume is planned.
- Royal Mail prices change periodically (last updated 7 April 2026 in this plan) — shipping charges should be reviewed against current rates before finalising listing prices.
