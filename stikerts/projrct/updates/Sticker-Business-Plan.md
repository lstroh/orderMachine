## 1. Executive Summary

This plan sets out a low-overhead side hustle producing custom vinyl stickers — starting with bin/wheelie bin number stickers, personalised kids' name labels, and simple vinyl decals (water bottles, laptops), sold via eBay first, then Etsy, then Amazon (own website at doorstepstudio.co.uk to follow — timing TBD). The business will start unregistered for VAT, staying below the UK's £90,000 VAT threshold, and will launch with a Tier 1 equipment setup (current-generation inkjet printer + cutting machine + cold roll laminator), which is confirmed as the genuine small-business production standard for this market, not a compromise.

## 2. Market Overview

**Bin Stickers**

An active UK market with multiple established sellers (Etsy, Stickythings, UK Sign Shop, Discount Sticker Printing). Demand is recurring — every household with a wheelie bin is a potential customer, and demand renews naturally as people move house.

**Kids' Name Labels / Clothing Labels**

A larger, well-established niche. Brands like Stikins and My Nametags are built entirely around this product, with Stikins alone holding over 13,000 five-star reviews. Demand is strongly seasonal (back-to-school peak) but highly repeat-purchase, since parents return yearly as children grow and school years change.

**Vinyl Decals (water bottles, laptops, general)**

One of Etsy's most consistently strong sticker categories. Top-selling listings report 10,000–50,000+ lifetime sales, at price points of $2.50–$5 per sticker or $12–$25 for multi-packs. Broad, evergreen demand not tied to a single season.

**Competitive Landscape**

Fragmented across many small sellers rather than dominated by a handful of large players — a realistic entry point for a new, well-priced, well-presented shop rather than a market that requires beating an established giant.

## 7. Packaging Strategy

Every order includes a thank-you card as standard (`thankyou_card.py`, see `thankyou_card_README.md`). **Since Oct 2026 there is one card for every order and channel:** a confetti front with "Thank you!" and the Doorstep Studio logo, and a six-step "How to apply your sticker" guide on the back, written for the peel-tab back cut. It carries no buyer name (the personal wrap already does that), no discount code and no review request. That keeps it inside the eBay, Etsy and Amazon insert rules on every channel. Never add a discount paired with a review request, or ask for a positive/5-star review. Reasons: eBay restricts promoting sales outside eBay and coded coupons need an eBay Shop subscription; Etsy codes must resolve on Etsy; Amazon treats insert review requests as manipulation risk.

**Card sizes match the envelope:** A6 for C6 (4 per A4 sheet), A5 landscape for C5 (2 per A4 sheet, confetti on the left, steps and a smoothing diagram on the right) and A4 for C4. Small-tier orders use A6.

**Peel markers (bin stickers, Oct 2026):** each sticker has a back cut 15mm below its top edge. A magenta "PEEL THIS FIRST" pill (34 × 9mm) sits in the middle of that strip, and a 12 × 14mm arrow sits about 3mm below the cut, pointing up at it. Neither may cross the cut, or the strip won't come away. Customers peel the strip first, so the pill goes in the bin with it. One set per sticker: 4 per Small pack, 2 per Medium, 1 per Large.

Packaging approach varies by product to match its use case and physical requirements:

| Product | Approach | Components | Cost/order |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decals & name labels | Premium (gift feel) | C6 kraft envelope + tissue wrap + thank-you card + self-made seal sticker | ≈ £0.34 |
| Bin stickers (140×100mm, 4-pack) | Rigid, functional, with a personal touch | A6/C6 board-backed "do not bend" envelope (114×162mm) + **personal paper wrap** + thank-you card + seal sticker under plant-based clear tape + peel markers on the back of each sticker | Envelope ≈ £0.25 + wrap £0.13 (hand-cut; £0.42 on the Cricut) + tape £0.06 + peel markers and back cut £0.34. Envelope ≈ £0.25 (sourced, box-of-250 scale, All Colour Envelopes). The cost sheet also counts the thank-you card separately (£0.16) — confirm the £0.25 does not already include it. The R&D test pack (Double Dragon, 50) works out at £0.147 each — reconcile which supplier will be used |
| Packaging labels (B2B) | Simple, professional | C6 kraft envelope + thank-you card | ≈ £0.21 |

Bin stickers require a board-backed envelope rather than a standard kraft mailer, to prevent creasing/folding in transit. This is a functional requirement, not an upsell. **Note:** the bin sticker size was reduced from the original 30×20cm to 140×100mm (see StickerBinStickersCosts_v13.xlsx, "Bin Stickers 100x140 4pk" sheet), so the envelope size dropped accordingly from **C4 to A6/C6** — smaller and cheaper than the original C4 assumption, though the per-unit cost happens to land in the same ≈£0.23–0.25 range. **A6/C6 applies to the 140×100mm baseline only:** the Medium (202×140mm) and Large (280×200mm) tiers (Section 12) are too big for it and would need roughly C5 and C4 board-backed envelopes respectively — not yet sourced or costed. The A6/C6 choice is confirmed but still to be validated in the post by Test 6 (transit stress, see Bin-Sticker-Material-Test-Plan.md).

**Personal wrap (bin stickers, Oct 2026):** each 4-pack is wrapped in a cross-folded 120gsm paper wrap made per order by `sticker_wrap.py` (run through `make_order.py`; see `sticker_wrap_README.md`). The front shows "Made especially for" with the customer's address and a half-size preview of their exact design, plus the Doorstep Studio logo. The back has the "Handmade Just For You" badge, "Thank you, {name}!", the care line and "Recycle me with your paper". It closes with a tab and slot, so it is reclosable, with no glue or tape. Cut by hand (PDF) or with Cricut Print Then Cut (PNG, 180 × 232mm). It adds about 4.1g and 0.5–0.6mm, which matters for the Letter limit and the box weight (see the Shipping Plan).

**Plastic-free closure:** the envelope is closed with the 40mm seal sticker, now printed on regular (non-waterproof) sticker paper, under a strip of Sellotape Zero Plastic (plant-based cellulose). Listings can accurately say "plastic-free packaging". The tape is compostable, not recyclable, so don't call the tape recyclable.

**Multi-pack orders (resolved Oct 2026 for the same product):** a single pack ships in the A6/C6 board-backed envelope (Letter, £0.91). Orders of 2–4 packs ship loose in the AKAR white A6/C6 Large Letter box (23mm external, about 84g with 4 packs and tape, Large Letter £1.55). Cap the box at 4 loose packs: a fifth would land near 97g, too close to the 100g band; with the personal wrap the cap drops to 3 (see below). Postage vs separate Letters: £0.27 saved at 2 packs, £1.18 at 3, £2.09 at 4; one pack is cheaper in the envelope. Box price is still to be added in the cost sheet (tape is in v7). **With wraps, 4 packs come to about 100g, the edge of the band — cap the box at 3 wrapped packs until 4 is weighed under 100g.** **Still open:** orders mixing different products (for example name labels plus bin stickers).

The seal sticker, wrap, thank-you card and peel markers are all printed in-house on the same printer. The seal sticker is printed on SmithPackaging A4 label sheets (£8.99 for 100, 24 stickers a sheet), under £0.01 each including ink. The peel markers use the same label sheets, 35 sets a sheet (user-tested Oct 2026), cut with Print Then Cut; about £0.014 a set including ink and a mat use.

## 8. Shipping Strategy

Competitor research shows a consistent pattern: free standard (untracked) shipping baked into the item price is the default across nearly every seller in this space (Stikins, wheelie bin sticker Etsy shops, My Nametags), with tracked delivery offered as a paid checkout upgrade. No competitor found offers a genuinely guaranteed next-day service — this is an open gap.

Stickers and labels are thin and flat, qualifying for Royal Mail's Large Letter format (up to 25mm thick) rather than Small Parcel — packaging should be kept flat to preserve this rate, since Small Parcel format roughly doubles postage cost. If the packed order measures under 5mm it may qualify for the cheaper Letter rate; the Test 6 postage-limit checks (wrapped-pack gauge reading, AKAR box weights, post-to-self) passed (Oct 2026) and the launch is on Letter postage; the weights without the wrap, the Post Office counter pass and the post-to-self result are logged in `TestTrackingSheet.xlsx`, but the exact thickness in mm and the wrapped weights are not. For eBay orders, postage labels are bought through eBay's Royal Mail label tool (decided Oct 2026); a Royal Mail Click & Drop business account also exists as a fallback and for other channels. The cost sheet keeps the address label at £0 (labels printed free via QR code at a Post Office or Royal Mail locker; Tracked Large Letter labels probably need home printing, as Post Office printing for Tracked is parcel-only). The eBay postage policy is set up (6 Oct 2026) with **Royal Mail 2nd Class Letter, free**. **To confirm at the first order:** that eBay's label tool offers untracked 2nd Class Letter at the £0.91 the cost sheet assumes (otherwise use Click & Drop for that label).

## 10. Production Workflow

Confirmed as the genuine professional small-business method for waterproof vinyl stickers:

1. Generate the artwork (bin_sticker.py per-size scripts for bin stickers; Cricut Design Space for other products), laid out multiple-per-sheet with corner tick marks.
2. Print onto printable vinyl using the pigment-ink printer (waterproof once dry — critical for durability). **Bin sticker material: Cricut Printable Waterproof Sticker Set – A4 (20 ct), White (vinyl + matched laminate) — chosen Sep 2026.** Cricut renders dark saturated colours such as navy as near-black (Test Plan, Addendum 5), so accent colours must be checked before they are offered.
3. Apply laminate using the cold roll laminator (squeegee by hand as backup), before cutting, to protect the print and make it water/scratch resistant.
4. Cut on the Cricut Explore 5 in **cut-only mode for all size tiers** (fixed rounded-rectangle outlines at known coordinates, no registration marks). Square the printed sheet on the mat by hand against the printed corner tick marks. All three tiers' cut areas (about 200–202mm × 280mm) fit the Explore 5's 12×12 mat (292.1mm maximum cut area): Small (140×100mm, 4-up), Medium (202×140mm, 2-up stacked) and Large (280×200mm, 1-up).
5. **Back cut (Oct 2026):** turn the sheet face down on the mat and run a second cut-only pass that slits the backing only (not the vinyl), 15mm below the top edge of each sticker. Square it against the corner ticks as for the front cut. Blade depth through the backing alone needs its own test cuts.
6. Trim the kiss-cut sheet into separate backed stickers on the guillotine (and cut thank-you cards to size), remove waste material.
7. **Peel markers (Oct 2026):** on the back of each sticker, stick a "PEEL THIS FIRST" pill in the middle of the 15mm strip and an arrow about 3mm below the cut, centred under the pill. Keep both clear of the cut. Markers are printed in batches, not per order: SmithPackaging label sheets, 35 sets a sheet, cut with Print Then Cut at 3.4 × 0.9cm (pill) and 1.2 × 1.4cm (arrow), lock on, scale 100%. Files: `peel_this_first_marker_34x9mm.png`, `peel_arrow_marker_12x14mm.png`.
8. Package (per the packaging strategy above) and ship.
9. **Personal wrap (small bin stickers, Oct 2026):** `make_order.py` makes the sticker sheet and one wrap per order from the same order list. Print the wrap on plain 100–120gsm paper (Epson driver: Plain Paper, or Presentation Paper Matte for coated paper), let it dry 5–10 minutes, then cut by hand or with Print Then Cut (180 × 232mm), and fold round the stack. Print Then Cut is used for the wrap, the seal stickers and the peel markers, which all fit its A4 limit; the sticker sheets stay cut-only.

*This replaces both Print-Then-Cut (its registration-mark area is too small for a full A4 sheet) and the earlier corner punch + Slice nick workflow; the guillotine stays for trimming. Kiss-cut depth (through vinyl and laminate, not the backing) needs test cuts on the actual stock.*

Estimated time for a 20-sticker batch: 45–70 minutes (estimate predates the cut-only workflow, the back cut pass and applying 2 markers per sticker — re-time once tested), with cutting taking the largest share. Time per batch compresses for repeat designs (same layout, personalised text/number swapped in).

**Planned — Premium Bin Sticker Tier (next, immediately after current bin sticker launch)**

## 13. Risks & Considerations

- Sticker market is competitive — differentiation through design quality, niche focus, and customer service matters.
- Ink/material choice is critical to durability — dye-based ink or skipping lamination risks poor-quality, smearing products and negative reviews.
- Turnover must be tracked against the VAT threshold on a rolling 12-month basis as the business scales.
- Seasonal products (back-to-school labels, wedding favours) will see demand fluctuate — a mixed product line smooths this out.
- **Unit margin at £5 is thin and postage-dependent: about £0.87 a pack before overhead at the Letter rate, and about £0.23 at Large Letter (Section 9, cost sheet v13).** The Letter rate (launch plan) is worth £0.64 a pack, so the Test 6 result still matters, and the fixed overhead (£83.22/month) is not covered by the small pack alone at realistic early volumes. The launch price is now set; overhead and the Promoted Listings question are the remaining margin levers.
- Royal Mail prices change periodically (last updated 7 April 2026 in this plan) — shipping charges should be reviewed against current rates before finalising listing prices.
