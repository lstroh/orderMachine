## ✅ Decided

**Product structure**
- Listing A: 13 solo designs, one listing, **Design** variation. Designs grouped into 3 style clusters (Wreaths & Florals / Classic & Formal / Minimal & Modern) for main image and description organisation — not split into separate listings at launch.
- Listing B: Animal Family packs (Duck/Dog/Cat), separate listing, **Species** variation. Fixed set of 4 different scenes per family (not buyer-configurable).
- Both listings adding **Size** as a second variation attribute *(launch timing for Medium/Large still open — see below)*: Small (4 stickers) / Medium (2 stickers) / Large (1 sticker) — fixed pairing, not independent size+quantity dropdowns like some competitors use.
- Base size stays 140×100mm (deliberate — 6 of 7 logged competitors use 150×100mm, flagged once, decision made to stay differentiated).

**Pricing & shipping**
- Small (4-pack) launch price: **£5.00 with Letter postage (decided Oct 2026)**. Profit before overhead ≈ **£0.87/pack** (cost sheet v13: £5.00 − £1.39 fees − £2.73 landed cost); ≈£0.23 if it ever shipped Large Letter. Medium and Large are priced separately later and will differ. The earlier £3.10 capped launch batch is dropped (it would lose about £0.63 a pack).
- Free shipping built into item price (not charged separately) — matches all 7 competitors, gets a Cassini ranking boost, fees calculated on total price either way so no fee difference.
- Multi-order discount: the listing form's **Multi-buy** option is available without a paid Shop (seen 6 Oct 2026) — use that rather than Order Size Discount. Percentage to be set once real combined-pack weights are confirmed. Multi-pack orders of the same product ship in the AKAR box (2–4 packs, Large Letter £1.55; cap 3 wrapped packs until the 4-pack weight is logged).
- Dispatch: 1 business day (same-day production, next-day ship), Sunday/bank holidays excluded from handling time.
- Default shipping: Royal Mail 2nd Class Letter, untracked, absorbed into price. **Labels: eBay's label tool for eBay orders (decided Oct 2026)**; the Click & Drop business account exists as a fallback. Still to confirm in eBay's tool at the first order: untracked Letter is offered at about £0.91 (a 2025 eBay community thread reports business sellers suddenly being offered only Large Letter and up — if that happens, buy that order's label in Click & Drop).
- **Launch with free 2nd Class only (decided 6 Oct 2026).** Tracked 48 and Next Working Day (Tracked 24) are deferred, to be added later as buyer-paid upgrades by editing the postage policy. Prices not yet set. Royal Mail raised Tracked prices on 5 Oct 2026: Tracked 48 Large Letter is now **£2.90** and Tracked 24 **£3.90** online (letters and large letters unchanged). Allowing for eBay fees on postage (about 15.8% incl. VAT, or 20.6% if the 4% promotion fee also applies to postage), the break-even upgrade charge is about **£2.37–2.51** (Tracked 48) and **£3.55–3.77** (Tracked 24); suggested **£2.69** and **£3.99**. The Business Plan's earlier examples (+£1.50–2.00 and +£3.00) would now lose money. Note: eBay Premium Service (only open to Top Rated sellers) needs an express option within 2 working days, which Tracked 48 does not count as.
- **Label printing:** Royal Mail says Post Office label printing for Tracked services is parcel-format only, so a Tracked Large Letter label would probably need printing at home. The owner printed a label at a Royal Mail locker via QR code (Oct 2026) — another free printing route (service used not recorded).

**Returns (decided Oct 2026)**
- eBay requires business sellers to accept returns, with 14 days as the shortest window, so the returns policy is **Returns accepted, 14 days, buyer pays return postage**. The change-of-mind exemption for personalised items comes from using eBay's Personalisation feature on the listing.
- 30-day returns only matter for eBay Premium Service, which first needs Top Rated status (90+ days active, 100+ transactions and £1,000+ sales in 12 months — about 250 packs at £5.00). Revisit then; editing the policy updates all listings at once.
- eBay's returns form has no refund-method or free-text field, so this wording goes in the **listing description**: *"As these stickers are personalised to order, they can't be returned for a change of mind. If your order arrives damaged, faulty, or with an error on our part, contact us and we'll replace or refund it."*

**Listing mechanics**
- Single listing per product line beats splitting — backed by real evidence (28% conversion-rate difference cited by one seller; eBay's algorithm rewards consolidated history).
- ~~Personalisation fallback: if the box is left blank, default to the buyer's checkout delivery address~~ — **dropped (Sep 2026)**. Reviewer feedback was split (2 of 3 said keep with safeguards, 1 said always message the buyer instead, given a wrong personalised item is unusable and unreturnable). Decision: no automatic fallback for now — message the buyer if the box is left blank. Revisit once there's real order volume to see how often this actually happens.
- eBay's per-variation pricing supports the Size tiers without needing separate listings.
- Revisit whether to split any design cluster into its own listing **4–8 weeks post-launch**, once ~20–30 sales exist, using Seller Hub Traffic reports (own-listing data only, not marketplace-wide).

**Tools**
- eBay's free Listings Assistant for a first-draft title/description/item specifics.
- ~~Canva (free tier) for image composites, size-comparison graphics, personalisation explainer.~~ **Dropped for eBay photos (Oct 2026)** — eBay's Picture Policy bans added text, artwork, marketing, borders, badges and watermarks on listing photos (see Photo plan below). Canva may still suit Etsy/own website, rules not yet checked.
- Photoroom — background clean-up on real photos. **Not yet adopted.** Free plan watermarks every export and bans commercial use, so only Pro is usable for eBay. Try eBay's free built-in background tool on the same photos first; subscribe only if it struggles (e.g. black bin edges). Cost sheet v6 has a £0 placeholder row.
- Midjourney — sticker artwork only. Not used to decorate or fake listing photos. Omni Reference checked and rejected for this use (V7-only, generates AI product scenes); see `Midjourney-Beginner-Guide.md` §8.
- Photopea (free) for Clone Stamp/Spot Healing touch-ups on real photos. ~~Perspective-warp compositing~~ — avoid compositing a sticker onto a bin it was never on; photos must accurately represent the item.
- eBay's native AI background-enhancement tool (iOS) for flat-lay clean-up.
- 3Dsellers / Frooition — cross-sell template tools, worth revisiting once both listings are live and can cross-promote each other; not needed at launch.
- Rejected: Kittl (wrong product category), Zeely.ai (billing complaint red flags, wrong use case).

**Competitor research**

**Separate listings per size — considered and rejected (Oct 2026)**
- Question: split sizes into separate listings so each size gets its own photos and main image?
- Evidence: eBay's own seller page recommends multi-variation listings; its listing performance score pools sales and impressions across all variations, and Best Match reads variation values (e.g. "Large") for search relevance. Seller anecdotes and a UK listing-tool company report the same direction (split listings dilute views, watchers and sales history). No controlled data found.
- The usual exception (split when the picture differs, e.g. colours) doesn't apply: the sizes look the same in photos, just bigger.
- Competitor scan (Oct 2026): sellers offering several sizes put them in one listing with a size dropdown (SticksyUK, MGB, C3 LaserEdge and others, up to 958 sold per listing); the biggest sellers (C1, C4–C7) mostly offer a single 150×100mm size; the only splitting found was per design, with low sales (3–65 sold). Not a controlled test.
- Decision (confirmed by user): keep sizes as a variation in one listing for now. Only Small exists at launch anyway. Revisit with own sales data, if eBay won't accept a one-value Size dropdown, or with new evidence. Moving a size out later (zero stock in the combined listing, own listing) is likely possible; check the duplicate rule at the time.
