## 3. Notes on the techniques that matter most

### Print Then Cut (rows 2–4)

- **A4 size cap is now confirmed by Cricut** (help article edited 19 Aug 2026, Explore series row): on A4 the maximum is **10.62in (26.98cm) vertical** and **7.2in (18.3cm) horizontal**. Cricut stresses that the cuttable area is not a full rectangle because of the sensor markings, so a design using the maximum in one direction may not reach the maximum in the other. Bigger paper sizes (up to A3) are supported by the machine, but the ET-3950 is A4-only.
- **Print in one Design Space session.** A PDF printed outside it can give wrongly sized sensor marks.
- **A4 goes on the mat landscape**, with the printed arrow top-left pointing up.
- **Print settings:** highest quality, no narrow margins, no scaling, no edge printing, no centre alignment.
- **Gloss warning:** Cricut says non-Cricut materials that are dark, high-gloss or holographic may cause sensor problems. Your Stickiply glossy vinyl is a non-Cricut gloss material, so it may struggle here. Cricut's own sets are the safe baseline.
- **Cricut's sticker-set rules:** inkjet only, Light Grip mat, choose the matching setting under "Browse All Materials", **do not use transfer tape** with the set, and it needs the more powerful sensor of Explore 3 or later (fine for the Explore 5).
- **Not relevant to cut-only:** the bin-sticker method has no sensor scan, so the size cap and gloss warning don't apply to it.

### Kiss-cutting and custom settings (rows 1 and 3)

- Cricut's "Create Sticker" flow offers **Kiss Cut** (cuts the sticker material and leaves the liner) and **Die Cut** with an optional easy-peel border. You can choose More or Less pressure on the cut screen. One third-party source (Feb 2026) says Create Sticker needs Cricut Access; I couldn't confirm that on Cricut's site, and cut-only doesn't need it.
- For stacks not in Design Space, use **Custom Material Settings**. Cricut says these can be created on desktop or iOS but **not on Android**, so do the tuning on a computer.
- Design Space has built-in presets for Cricut's sticker sets (on the Maker 5 list: "White" and "Laminated Printable Sticker Paper, White", both with 2× multi-cut). I couldn't read the Explore 5's own list, so treat those as starting references only.
- Cricut's suggested ceiling is **under 2.0mm thick** for Explore machines.

### Cut vinyl and iron-on (rows 5–7)

- Smart Materials load **without a mat**, need at least 6in of length, and the machine measures it for you.
- Smart Materials cut width on the Explore 5: Cricut's Smart Materials article says up to 11.7in, while the Explore 5 Quick Start FAQ says 12in. Plan on 11.7in. On a mat, Cricut's mat limits (11.5in) apply.
- Your Smart Vinyl and Iron-On came as **9.5×13in sheets**, so long decals need rolls. Permanent Smart Vinyl rolls (3ft, 12ft, 21ft) exist. **The Roll Holder is listed by Cricut for Explore 3/4 and Maker 3/4 only, not Explore 5**, so check before buying rolls.
- Cut permanent vinyl could be a **second bin-number product** (cut vinyl instead of printed and laminated). That's an idea only: it needs its own cost sheet and an outdoor durability check.
- The EasyPress Mini LT suits small designs (Cricut markets it for baby clothes, tees and small gifts). Anything bigger needs a larger press.

### Card, scoring and drawing (rows 8–11)

- Your 250gsm card sits between Cricut's medium (216gsm) and heavy (270gsm) cardstock categories, so expect to test it.
- You have Light Grip mats only. Cricut sells four grip strengths and Design Space suggests one per material, so heavier card may want a firmer mat. Not verified.
- Scoring needs a manual tool swap each job (third-party review), which is fine for occasional cards but slows batch work.

### Selling products made with Cricut's library content

Cricut's own images and fonts are covered by its Angel Policy, which sources describe as allowing finished physical products (with a cap of 10,000 units a year, a "Includes Copyright Materials of Cricut" notice, and licensed characters excluded). One third-party source (Feb 2026) says the policy was updated and tells sticker sellers to use their own designs. **Your designs from `bin_sticker.py` avoid the issue.** Check Cricut's current terms before using any library image or font on something you sell.

## 4. What we can't do right now (confirmed)

| Limit | Why | Workaround / future option |
|---|---|---|
| **Cut raw fabric, tissue, crepe paper** | Needs the Rotary Blade, Maker-only | Bonded (backed) fabric works with the Bonded-Fabric Blade; otherwise a Maker |
| **Cut wood, balsa, mat board, heavy chipboard, thick leather, foam core** | Knife Blade is Maker-only; Explore is limited to about 2.0mm | Deep Cutting Tool handles thicker card, foam, rubber and magnet, not wood |
| **Engrave, deboss, perforate, wavy-cut** | QuickSwap tips are Maker-only | Maker 4/5 |
| **Use the old Scoring Stylus** | Explore 5 uses the new Scoring Tool | Use the bundled tool |
| **Use older Explore pens unless marked with the universal symbol** | Redesigned pen holder | Buy compatible pens |
| **Print white** | ET-3950 has no white ink | Use white or coloured vinyl, or the White Acrylic marker for touches |
| **Print Then Cut a full A4 sheet** | Sensor markings reduce the cuttable area (10.62 × 7.2in max) | Cut-only for rectangles |
| **Infusible Ink / sublimation** | The EasyPress Mini LT isn't compatible | A larger EasyPress (not checked) |
| **Cut food or fondant** | Not food-safe | — |
| **Long decals from sheets** | Only 9.5×13in sheets owned | Buy Smart Vinyl rolls |

## 6. Suggested first tests when the machine arrives

1. Register the machine, update Design Space, and check the material list for the Explore 5 (this is the one thing I couldn't read online).
2. Cut one Smart Vinyl decal in a bundle colour to learn the weeding and transfer routine.
3. Press one Smart Iron-On design with the EasyPress Mini LT on scrap fabric, using Cricut's Heat Guide.
4. Print Then Cut one sticker on the bundle's Printable Vinyl (Cricut's own material).
5. Repeat on the Cricut White set with laminate, then on Stickiply glossy, to see whether gloss affects the sensors.
6. Create a custom material setting on a computer for the laminated stack (Addendum 6, Part 2).
7. Cut a scored card from your 250gsm white and kraft card.
8. Try the White Acrylic and Gold markers on kraft.
