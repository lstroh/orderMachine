## C3 — LaserEdge Designs UK — Personalised Bin Number Stickers for Wheelie Bins Decal Vinyl 1/3/4 D1

- **URL:** ebay.co.uk/itm/227217044136
- **Price:** £2.99 **each** — critical structural difference, see below
- **Postage:** Free, Royal Mail 2nd Class Letter (absorbed into price)

**Seller:**
- LaserEdge Designs UK (laseredgedesignsuk) — registered business seller
- 98.2% positive feedback, 41K items sold
- Joined May 2022 (4 years trading)
- Wider workshop specialises in laser-cut MDF/acrylic, personalised gifts, signage

**⚠️ Pricing structure is fundamentally different from ours and C1 — priced per sticker, not per pack:**
"Each quantity = 1 complete decal set (your number + your street name)." Buying quantity 4 for four bins costs **~£11.96 total**, nearly 3x more than our entire proposed 4-pack at £3-4. With 41K items sold and strong feedback, this is real evidence of demand at roughly **£3/single sticker** — worth weighing seriously against our own £3-4/pack-of-4 assumption; we may be underpricing relative to proven market willingness-to-pay in this exact category.

**Smart mechanism — multi-bin/multi-address ordering solved simply:** buyer increases quantity for multiple sets, then lists each different address in the personalisation/message box. Solves the "different address per bin" problem within eBay's native tools, no custom variation logic needed. Worth adopting if a customer ever wants multiple addresses in one order.

**Smart production approach — max-bounding-box sizing, not fixed dimensions:** Small (max 14×14cm) / Large (max 19×19cm). Design auto-scales to fit long street names within the bounding box while staying proportional, rather than a single fixed size. Worth checking whether `bin_sticker.py` already handles long-street-name overflow this way or needs this logic added.

**Item specifics used:** Condition, Pattern, Shape, Size, Material, Additional Parts Required, Orientation, Customised, Suitable For, Subject, Colour, Brand, Department, Type, Care Instructions, Theme, Style, Features, Room, Country of Origin, Personalise.
- **Note:** several fields are clearly wrong/leftover template values ("Shape: Bottle," "Suitable For: Window," "Department: Children") — not tailored to this product. Despite this, seller has strong sales volume, suggesting item-specifics precision matters less to buyers than assumed — though still worth doing properly ourselves.

**Description structure:**
- **Branded header banner** at the top (logo, gradient background, seller name + tagline) — nice polish touch, cheap to replicate in Canva.
- Overview → Quantity & Colours (with worked examples) → Size & Personalising (bounding-box explanation) → Text Format guidance → Postage note (recommends Express if urgent) → numbered Application Instructions → Care Instructions → "Where You Can Use These Decals."
- Explicit warning: "Orders without details may be delayed or refunded to prevent late dispatch" — direct, protects seller from incomplete orders.

**Photos (2 total):**
1. Four different coloured wheelie bins (black/green/brown/blue) side by side against a real brick wall with hanging baskets and paving — reads as genuine styled photography, the most convincing "real photo" look of the three competitors seen so far. Bold text overlay: "BIN STICKERS — PACK OF 1,3 OR 4."
2. Single close-up bin (grey) with the same overlay text, tighter crop on the sticker itself.

**Strengths:** most realistic/polished photography seen yet, clever bounding-box sizing solves a real production edge case, branded description header, multi-address ordering solved simply, direct proof of demand at a meaningfully higher per-unit price point.

**Weaknesses / gaps:** sloppy/wrong item specifics fields, per-sticker pricing may look expensive at a glance next to a "pack of 4 for £X" framing (worth testing which framing actually converts better — this is unresolved, not necessarily a weakness).
