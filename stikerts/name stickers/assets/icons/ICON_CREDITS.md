## What you must do

- **Game Icons (CC BY 3.0):** credit the author whenever you sell a label using one of these icons. Put the credit line for that theme (below) in the listing description, and ideally on the instructions card too. The licence allows changes such as recolouring; the credit should say the icon was recoloured.
- **Fluent Emoji High Contrast (Microsoft, MIT)**, **Material Design Icons (Pictogrammers, Apache 2.0)**, **Phosphor Icons (MIT)**, **Tabler Icons (MIT)** and **MingCute Icons (Apache 2.0):** no credit needed on the product or listing. Keep this file as your record of where they came from.
- All six licences allow use on products you sell, with no unit cap.
- Licence texts: [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), [MIT (Fluent Emoji)](https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji/blob/main/LICENSE), [Apache 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0), [MIT (Phosphor)](https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core/blob/main/LICENSE), [MIT (Tabler)](https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons/blob/main/LICENSE), [Apache 2.0 (MingCute)](https://github.com/Richard9394/MingCute/blob/main/LICENSE).

## How the files were made

Each icon was taken from the Iconify npm packages (`@iconify-json/game-icons`, `fluent-emoji-high-contrast`, `mdi`, `ph`, `tabler`, `mingcute`), rendered as solid black on a transparent background at 800px on the long side, and cropped with a 2% margin. `name_sticker.py` recolours them to the label accent. Game Icons authors were confirmed against the author folders in the official game-icons/icons repository on GitHub.
